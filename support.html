NoMedia Support

NoMedia hides images and videos in Safari on the sites you choose.

Turn it on

  1. Install NoMedia from the App Store and open the app once.
  2. In Safari, open Settings, then Extensions, and turn NoMedia on.
  3. Allow NoMedia to see the websites you want it to change.
  4. Reload the page you are viewing.

Private Browsing is a separate switch on that same Extensions screen. NoMedia stays off in Private Browsing until you allow it.

If pictures are still showing

  • Click the NoMedia button in the Safari toolbar and check that the site is set to hide.
  • Reload the page after you change a setting.
  • Files you open from your Mac, iPhone, or iPad stay unchanged. Safari does not let an extension hide media in those files.
